## 24-Hour Claim

The old site copy used "24ωρο delivery". This is not staying as prominent live copy.

Why it appeared:

- Instagram search snippet for `@crema_gazi` says "24hr delivery crepes-waffles-coffee-pastry shop".
- Facebook search snippet includes "ωρες delivery 24ωρο efood deal & Wolt".
- Tripadvisor shows Crema open 12:00 AM - 11:59 PM every day.
- Restaurant Guru shows "Open 24 hours" and lists every day as open 24 hours.

Decision:

- Keep the website copy focused on confirmed positioning: creperie, coffee, waffles, sweets, delivery, Persefonis 63, phone, menu/order links.
- Menu-specific copy can mention Wolt-visible categories such as sweet/savory crepes, My Waffle, croissant, puff pastries, sandwiches, sweets, ice cream, coffees and beverages.
- Avoid using "24ωρο delivery" as a hero/footer claim unless the owner confirms it directly or we decide to cite the public listings explicitly in a business-info section.
